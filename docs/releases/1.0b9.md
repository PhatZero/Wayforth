## WayForth Forever 1.0b9

- Profession guides now provide ordered leveling checkpoints and remind you to train the next rank before reaching the current skill cap.
- Live recipe recommendations prioritize known orange recipes, then yellow recipes, and show materials required for one craft alongside bag counts.
- Recommendations recalculate after skill-ups to help avoid spending materials on recipes that no longer give useful points.
- Dashboard category cards now fit the available width, and Recently Used entries have clearer spacing.
